FIELD MANUAL // HOW TO PLAY

Follow radio messages from Stella and verified clues to reach Haven by DAY 100. Shelter stops advance the journey and provide rest and supplies. Road encounters vary between runs, while your choices determine your route and ending. Choices spend time and change your odds, health, radiation, food, water, and luck. Food and water drain as you travel; forage or choose resource-rich paths before shortages damage your health. Difficulty changes your starting edge and daily drain.

Read each scene, choose an action, then review its result before selecting Continue onward. Signed amounts next to your stats show gains and losses until your next click.

Health reaching zero ends the run. Relationships and major decisions shape the ending. Romance is optional. Restart Run starts over; refreshing the page also resets your current progress.
